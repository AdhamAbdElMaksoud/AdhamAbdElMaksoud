# Hi, I'm Adham Abd El-Maksoud 👋

**Senior Retail Merchandiser & Data Analyst** · Maadi, Cairo, Egypt

I turn daily sales and stock data into decisions that put the right product in the right store at the right time.

### What I do
- **Stock allocation** from the warehouse to stores, based on each store's capacity
- **Replenishment** of sold-out items, weekly and by season
- **Store rotations** to fix broken size runs
- **New-collection floor sets** and stock for new store openings
- **Core-item tracking**: sell-through, weeks of cover and reorders
- **Purchase orders**: placing them and following them up

### What I build
- Daily **HTML reports** generated automatically with Python
- **Power BI** dashboards and **Excel / Power Pivot** models (DAX, Power Query)
- Offers and promotions analysis
- Automation scripts that turn hours of manual work into minutes
